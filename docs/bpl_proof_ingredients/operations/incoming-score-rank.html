    Operation · §10.2

    Recover the padded incoming score rank

    Manuscript: §10.2, p. 41.

    Physical queries

    • P-INCOMINGRANK Recover an incoming rank from score contours

    An explicit reference enumerates the completed score column, quantizes the scores at the certificate place, and takes its padded rank. The physical version counts matching initials by traversing the score graph for each descending payload. The rank modes of P-INCOMING expose its digits, and its gate mode applies the cutoff after the rank has been defined.

    Physical routine

    P-INCOMINGRANK Recover an incoming rank from score contours

    \(\Call{P-INCOMINGRANK}(\mathcal T,\mathrm{kind},t)\longrightarrow(b)\)

    Return a canonical digit of the zero-padded incoming rank: \(k_c\) for incoming-\(I\) or \(j_{\rm det}\) for a pilot detector. At certificate place \(r\), each port is scored by T-SCORE's fixed quantization. The result is the \(k\)th largest quantized score, padded by zero, with all scores in the uncapped relation counted.
