    Operation · §5.2

    Compose the exact stages

    Manuscript: §5.2, p. 12.

    Logical reference

    • L-HIERARCHY The exact reward hierarchy

    The hierarchy uses the pilot, correction, and split at each stage. Its primary-copy reward is the deterministic quantity that the sampled reward vector estimates.

    Logical routine

    L-HIERARCHY The exact reward hierarchy

    \(\Call{L-HIERARCHY}(S,e,L,\Omega)\longrightarrow(w_L,z_L)\)

    The seed law and all constants are fixed once for the whole hierarchy. Each new \(\Omega_l\) is the same law evaluated on the new legal names, not a fresh randomness choice. Return the entire final reward and the all-primary descendant of the input start. The explicit input \(\Omega\) covers the finite name universe at every level; \(\Omega_l\) is only its stored subtable for level \(l\).
