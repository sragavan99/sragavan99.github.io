    Operation · §3.2

    Walk on the conditioned seed set

    Manuscript: §3.2, p. 8.

    Physical queries

    • P-SEEDSTEP One step of the conditional finite action
    • P-WORD Endpoint-independent word variants

    D-EXPANDER specifies a walk that respects required hits and frozen bits. D-ACTION and D-SLOTS give the concrete candidate. A fixed word can be represented by affine maps and candidate wraps before the endpoints are available. The valid wrap is identified by a later test. This allows the word description to be held during recursive evaluation.

    Physical routine

    P-SEEDSTEP One step of the conditional finite action

    \(\Call{P-SEEDSTEP}(\sigma,H,g,z,\mathrm{mode})\longrightarrow(\sigma^+,g^{-1},b)\)

    The 512 generator slots and conditional-factor layout are fixed in D-SLOTS. Modes RAW and TEST use a fixed wrap \(z\); RAW returns its full-space bijection without testing unknown endpoints, and TEST additionally returns its exact validity bit. Inverse mode uses the inverse affine map of that same fixed variant. Free auxiliary coordinates are selected by the public mask, never by their values.

    Physical routine

    P-WORD Endpoint-independent word variants

    \(\Call{P-WORD}(\sigma,H,w,\mathrm{mode})\longrightarrow(\sigma^+,b,\omega)\)

    \(w\) is a complete word/variant description: each of its \(h\) slots carries a 512-letter index and the bounded channel wrap. Return its resulting seed, conjunction of tests in TEST mode, and weight \(\omega=512^{-h}\). RAW replay is endpoint independent. Reverse mode inverts the fixed variant, not an adaptively selected wrap.
