    Operation · §6.1

    Normalize the ideal sampled tables

    Manuscript: §6.1, p. 13.

    Logical reference

    • L-IDEAL An ideal normalized sampled row

    At a magnitude scale the number of substantial entries in a row is bounded by its mass divided by that scale. Sampling gives a bounded expected degree. The gates enforce a bound on every seed and change the mean. The ideal table divides by the conditional mean of the relevant gates. This calculation supplies a comparison target for accuracy.

    Logical routine

    L-IDEAL An ideal normalized sampled row

    \(\Call{L-IDEAL}(d,g,u,a,\tau,\Omega,\theta)\longrightarrow(F)\)

    The inputs are the deterministic normalized entries \(d_{xy}\), a column multiplier, rates and an explicit seed/hit table. Return the full ideal normalized table at every seed. The conditional denominator includes precisely its numerator gates. This is an unbiasedness comparison object, not the implemented estimator.
