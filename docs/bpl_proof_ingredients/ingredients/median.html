    Ingredient · §12

    Median of multiple trials

    Manuscript: §12, from p. 46.

    The big picture

    Map ingredient 10 · toy API

    Enumerate seeds; select a median without storing the estimates.

    Input
    An enumerable family of finite-precision estimates, with a strict majority within the desired error.
    Output
    A deterministic estimate with that error guarantee.
    Upstream purpose
    Remove dependence on selecting a good seed.
    Approach
    Recompute trials while counting values below candidate thresholds. Toy the estimates 0, 0.49, 0.50, 0.51, 1 have median 0.50; repeated scans find it without storing the list.

    Improves

    Reliance on a good seed

    Introduces or worsens

    None recorded.

    §12.1 Evaluate one trial for each admitted seed

    The finishing law conditions only on the hit at the primary start. Each admitted seed produces one grid numerator by streaming the final reward and clipping the result. The full-space seed enumeration retains the multiplicity of original fair-bit strings.

    Physical queries

    • P-TRIAL Evaluate one seed on a fixed grid

    What to prove

    Check that enumeration realizes the single conditioned law in D-ROOTLAW and that each trial implements the fixed stream and grid truncation.

    §12.2 Select the lower median by scans

    Each scan uses the same sequence of trials. Counting values at or below each grid threshold suffices to return the lower median.

    Physical queries

    • P-MEDIAN Median by repeated finite scans

    What to prove

    Use the root moment bound under the enumerated law and the hierarchy remainder to put a majority of grid values within the target error. Check the space of one trial together with the seed and median counters.
