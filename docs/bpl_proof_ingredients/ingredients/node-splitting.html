    Ingredient · §5

    Node splitting

    Manuscript: §5, from p. 12.

    The big picture

    Map ingredient 2 · toy API

    Divide transition weights among new nodes with controlled growth.

    Input
    A graph whose remaining heavy edges enter a small set of flagged vertices.
    Output
    An equivalent graph with smaller individual weights and controlled node growth.
    Upstream purpose
    Handle heavy edges at destinations where shortcutting was suppressed.
    Approach
    Duplicate a vertex's future behaviour and divide incoming weights among its copies. Toy four copies turn an incoming weight of 0.8 into four weights of 0.2.

    Improves

    Large edge weights

    Introduces or worsens

    Dense rows; High-indegree vertices

    Used by tasks

    Task 1: Approximating matrix powers with a sequence of corrector matrices; Task 3: Compute an O(16ⁱ)-sparse Eᵢ in low space

    In the physical algorithm

    This ingredient has no physical routines of its own. Its corrections and copies enter the physical algorithm through the task families: the pilot and actual corrections and the residual copies in D-ROWFAMILIES and D-CHILD, whose fields P-CHILD returns and whose sample terms P-INTRINSICVALUE evaluates.

    §5.1 Divide the remaining large weights

    When a column's correction is restricted, some residual entries can still be large. Route those entries among copies, and apply the same projection to the reward. The copy sample in L-FAMILY and the copy branch of T-INTRINSIC implement the corresponding queries.

    What to prove

    Check the projection identity, the new entry bound, and the count of active copies.
