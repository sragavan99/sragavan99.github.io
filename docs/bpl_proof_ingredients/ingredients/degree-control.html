    Ingredient · §6

    Controlling worst-case degree

    Manuscript: §6, from p. 13.

    The big picture

    Map ingredient 4 · toy API

    Suppress crowded rows and columns; compensate for lost contributions.

    Input
    A sampled graph, a degree cap, and exact conditional retention probabilities bounded away from zero.
    Output
    Bounded row and column degrees on every seed, with the intended conditional entry means.
    Upstream purpose
    Guarantee bounded access and prevent one correlated error from being reused too often.
    Approach
    Suppress crowded rows and columns, then compensate using each contribution's own retention probability. Toy a contribution of 0.6 surviving with probability 3/4 becomes 0.8 when retained and zero otherwise; its mean remains 0.6.

    Improves

    High-indegree vertices; Dense rows; Accumulating correlated errors

    Introduces or worsens

    Numerical precision issues

    Used by tasks

    Task 3: Compute an O(16ⁱ)-sparse Eᵢ in low space; Task 4: Sparsifying from O(16ⁱ) to O(1) in low space

    §6.1 Normalize the ideal sampled tables

    At a magnitude scale the number of substantial entries in a row is bounded by its mass divided by that scale. Sampling gives a bounded expected degree. The gates enforce a bound on every seed and change the mean. The ideal table divides by the conditional mean of the relevant gates. This calculation supplies a comparison target for accuracy.

    Logical reference

    • L-IDEAL An ideal normalized sampled row

    What to prove

    Bound the normalizer away from zero where it is used and check the conditional mean after dividing by it.

    §6.2 Assemble final values and ports

    For the computed table, first finish all raw rows of the task. Their local scores define the incoming rank. A correction column that fails the port count loses the entire column of ports. These operations define the input relation for later products. The implementation exposes a row by port and digit queries.

    Logical reference

    What to prove

    Show that the fixed classes cover the gated support, that the cap deletes only zero gated values, and that each query agrees with the corresponding finite test or value. Check that successful pairs are inverse and that failure and cleanup restore all required state.
