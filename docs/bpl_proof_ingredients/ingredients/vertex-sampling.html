    Ingredient · §3

    Vertex sampling and conditional mixing

    Manuscript: §3, from p. 7.

    The big picture

    Map ingredient 3 · toy API

    Sample vertices; mix seeds while preserving required inclusions.

    Input
    A vertex set and a desired sampling rate.
    Output
    A seeded sampler and a rapidly mixing seed walk with constantly many choices per step. A fixed seed and rate give repeatable membership decisions without storing the sampled set.
    Upstream purpose
    Average sampled computations using short walk descriptions. The full construction also needs this mixing while designated end points remain sampled; the toy setting omits that conditioning.
    Approach
    Use a seed-homomorphic hash with a rapidly mixing walk on the seed space. Here, seed-homomorphic means that simple seed updates implement corresponding transformations of all hash outputs. Affine hashing supplies those transformations; relative property (T) establishes a spectral gap uniform in the seed-space size and sampling rate. Toy sampling 16 neighbours at rate 1/16 gives one neighbour in expectation, and walking between seeds varies that sample. Ingredient 5 decides how much averaging to perform.

    Improves

    Dense rows

    Introduces or worsens

    Accumulating correlated errors; Space footprint of addresses and walk descriptions; Reliance on a good seed

    Used by tasks

    Task 3: Compute an O(16ⁱ)-sparse Eᵢ in low space; Task 4: Sparsifying from O(16ⁱ) to O(1) in low space

    §3.1 Sample vertices at a prescribed rate

    Each filter is a membership predicate on a vertex name. The source, destination, and midpoint roles use separate seeds. The next product can use a previous task's source or destination filter as its midpoint filter, with the role and rate changes in D-CHILD. The finite field and hit law are specified in D-SEEDS.

    Physical queries

    • P-MEMBER Affine vertex membership

    What to prove

    Establish the hit probabilities and the conditional pair and count estimates for the actual nonzero-slope sampler.

    §3.2 Walk on the conditioned seed set

    D-EXPANDER specifies a walk that respects required hits and frozen bits. D-ACTION and D-SLOTS give the concrete candidate. A fixed word can be represented by affine maps and candidate wraps before the endpoints are available. The valid wrap is identified by a later test. This allows the word description to be held during recursive evaluation.

    Physical queries

    • P-SEEDSTEP One step of the conditional finite action
    • P-WORD Endpoint-independent word variants

    What to prove

    For use in the estimator, prove the exact word lift, weight preservation, and identification of the conditional law at each caller.

    §3.3 Validate a sampled arrival

    First establish that the candidate endpoint exists. Then test the required hits and wraps at the original source and destination. The P-ENDPOINT hit and wrap modes perform the local tests during the visits from Section §9.

    Physical queries

    • P-VALID Validate original hits and all deferred wraps

    What to prove

    Check that the tests implement the word and hit conditions for both signs of a budget difference, with restoration on every failed test.
