MapToy APIs

Click an ingredient to switch it on or off; right-click it (long-press on a touch screen, Shift+F10 from the keyboard) to see its toy API. Faded boxes are off. A task is on when all its ingredients are on. An obstacle is on when not all of its improving ingredients are on, and either it has no worsening ingredients or at least one of them is on. An arrow is shown when its ingredient is on.
