    Finite algebra used by the logical routines

    Manuscript: §A, from p. 50.

    In the physical algorithm

    This ingredient has no physical routines of its own. Its corrections and copies enter the physical algorithm through the task families: the pilot and actual corrections and the residual copies in D-ROWFAMILIES and D-CHILD, whose fields P-CHILD returns and whose sample terms P-INTRINSICVALUE evaluates.
